## About the website and product

### What is DrillBooks?

DrillBooks sells downloadable digital educational materials designed for independent practice and learning. Product files may include Jupyter notebooks, exercises, datasets, hints, examples, instructions, and solutions. DrillBooks states that no physical goods are shipped. [Home](https://www.drillbooks.codes/) · [Terms](https://www.drillbooks.codes/terms.html)

### What is Python for Data Roles?

Python for Data Roles is a practice-first product for learning practical Python and data-analysis skills through structured notebook exercises and realistic business scenarios. Its stated audience includes career switchers, students and graduates, and junior analysts. The website describes 500+ structured exercises and practice in native Jupyter notebooks. [Product page](https://www.drillbooks.codes/)

### Who is the product for?

- **Career switchers:** People moving from areas such as marketing, operations, or finance into data analytics.
- **Students and graduates:** People seeking practical data workflows and practice relevant to assignments or take-home exercises.
- **Junior analysts:** People who want to move repetitive reporting from Excel toward Python, Pandas, and visualization.

These are the audiences described on the product page; the site does not claim that the product guarantees a job, interview, promotion, income, or exam result. [Product page](https://www.drillbooks.codes/) · [Terms](https://www.drillbooks.codes/terms.html)

### Is it suitable for an absolute beginner?

The site says Module 1 starts with variables and data types. It also advises complete beginners to be comfortable consulting Python documentation or free syntax references while solving early questions. [FAQ](https://www.drillbooks.codes/)

### Does the product guarantee employment or a particular result?

No. DrillBooks says the products are for educational and practice purposes and do not guarantee employment, interviews, promotions, examination results, income, or any particular professional outcome. [Terms](https://www.drillbooks.codes/terms.html)

## Learning method and features

### What is the 10-question rule?

The product page describes each topic as a sequence of ten questions. Questions 1–3 are warm-ups, 4–6 are intermediate, 7–9 are advanced, and question 10 is a “Boss Fight”: an unstructured stakeholder-style request that asks the learner to apply the topic to a business problem. [Product page](https://www.drillbooks.codes/)

### What are the difficulty levels?

- **Questions 1–3 — Warm-up:** Basic syntax and fundamental library calls.
- **Questions 4–6 — Intermediate:** Combine concepts.
- **Questions 7–9 — Advanced:** Use multi-step logic, chained operations, and reasoning.
- **Question 10 — Boss Fight:** Tackle an unstructured stakeholder request.

### What does “output-only hint” mean?

The website says learners can see the expected output before revealing the expert solution code. The hint is intended to guide the learner without immediately showing the code. [Product page](https://www.drillbooks.codes/)

### Can I work in Jupyter on my own computer?

Yes. The product is delivered as native `.ipynb` notebook files. The site says learners can download the notebooks and work locally in JupyterLab, Jupyter Notebook, Anaconda, or VS Code with the Python and Jupyter extensions. [Product page](https://www.drillbooks.codes/) · [Download Help](https://www.drillbooks.codes/download-help.html)

### Do I need to watch video tutorials?

The product page presents DrillBooks as practice-first and emphasizes writing code in notebooks. It does not publish a claim that videos are included; do not promise video lessons.

## Curriculum

The site groups the curriculum into five master modules and two phases. [Product page](https://www.drillbooks.codes/)

### 1. Python Fundamentals — Phase 1

Variables and data types, operators, strings, control flow, loops, functions, error handling, and file input/output.

### 2. Data Structures and Modules — Phase 1

Lists, dictionaries, tuples, sets, date/time, and regular expressions.

### 3. NumPy — Numerical Python — Phase 1

Array fundamentals, indexing and slicing, array operations, statistical functions, array manipulation, and the random module.

### 4. Pandas Data Manipulation — Phase 2

DataFrame basics, selecting and filtering, data cleaning, transformations, GroupBy and aggregation, merging and joining, DateTime, reshaping, and Pandas statistics.

### 5. Data Visualization — Phase 2

Matplotlib topics include relational, categorical, distribution, composition, and multi-plot dashboards. Seaborn topics include relational, categorical, distribution, matrix, and figure-level multi-plots.

## Industry scenarios and datasets

### Are the datasets real?

The FAQ says the datasets are **realistic synthesized datasets**, not necessarily real records. They are designed to reproduce challenges found in professional data work, such as null values, inconsistent formats, text-cleaning problems, and date issues. [FAQ](https://www.drillbooks.codes/)

### What retail or e-commerce scenarios are included?

The site describes e-commerce, customer, sales, and inventory data. Example analysis tasks include customer lifetime value, finding high-value customers at risk, seasonal purchases, and inventory or product performance. It identifies Supply Chain and Marketing Analyst as relevant role contexts. [Product page](https://www.drillbooks.codes/)

### What Banking and BFSI scenarios are included?

The site describes transactions, customer risk profiles, credit behavior, and time-series data. Example tasks include anomalous transaction detection, loan-default risk, strict Boolean risk thresholds, and rolling financial metrics. It identifies Risk and Financial Data Analyst as relevant role contexts. [Product page](https://www.drillbooks.codes/)

### What Healthcare scenarios are included?

The site describes patient and hospital operations data, including inconsistent and missing records. Example tasks include patient wait times, 30-day readmissions, resource utilization, and clinical-record cleanup. It identifies Healthcare Operations Analyst as a relevant role context. [Product page](https://www.drillbooks.codes/)

### What is the Boss Fight example?

The website previews `Boss_Fight_Retail.ipynb`, Python 3, Question 10, with the prompt “Find high-value customers at risk.” It asks learners to segment customers by total revenue, purchase frequency, and days since their most recent order. [Product page](https://www.drillbooks.codes/)

## Requirements and setup

### What software do I need?

The FAQ lists Python 3.9 or higher and one of the following: JupyterLab, Jupyter Notebook, Anaconda, or VS Code with the Python and Jupyter extensions. [FAQ](https://www.drillbooks.codes/)

### What computer specifications are recommended?

The FAQ says at least 4 GB RAM is required and 8 GB is recommended. It does not publish more detailed processor, disk-space, or graphics-card requirements. [FAQ](https://www.drillbooks.codes/)

### Which operating systems are supported?

The product page lists Windows, macOS, and Linux. [Product page](https://www.drillbooks.codes/)

### How do I open a notebook file?

DrillBooks files generally use the `.ipynb` extension. Open them from JupyterLab, Jupyter Notebook, or VS Code with the Jupyter extension; a basic text editor is not recommended for normal use. A working Python installation and the packages named in the product instructions may be required. [Download Help](https://www.drillbooks.codes/download-help.html)

### What if Python says a package is missing?

Follow the included setup instructions and install the required packages in the same Python environment used by the notebook kernel. When contacting support, include the exact error and operating system, but remove passwords, private keys, personal paths, and other sensitive information. [Download Help](https://www.drillbooks.codes/download-help.html)

### Is a particular amount of free disk space listed?

The public FAQ does not specify a disk-space requirement. The product is delivered as a ZIP of notebooks and data files; check the download size shown at delivery and leave enough room to download and extract it. The advice to leave room is practical guidance, not a published minimum requirement.

## Access, licence, and portfolio use

### Do I receive lifetime access?

The FAQ says purchase is a one-time payment and that, once access is granted, the customer can download the ZIP and repeat the notebooks for refreshers. The Product Licence clarifies that “lifetime access” means the right to use the lawfully downloaded copy does not expire merely with time; it does **not** guarantee perpetual hosting, an active original download link, unlimited redelivery, future updates, or lifelong technical support. Keep a secure backup. [FAQ](https://www.drillbooks.codes/) · [Product Licence](https://www.drillbooks.codes/license.html)

### Can I share the files with a friend, class, or team?

No. Each retail purchase is intended for one individual. The licence prohibits sharing the files or download link, uploading them to file-sharing services, reselling, sublicensing, or using one purchase for a classroom, company, coaching centre, or team. Schools and teams should contact DrillBooks about licensing. [Product Licence](https://www.drillbooks.codes/license.html)

### Can I use my completed work in a portfolio?

The FAQ allows showcasing your own completed analysis, commentary, and visualizations, but prohibits redistributing DrillBooks exercise files, datasets, or proprietary solution notebooks. [FAQ](https://www.drillbooks.codes/) · [Product Licence](https://www.drillbooks.codes/license.html)

### Who owns my exercise work?

The licence says you own original code and written material you independently create while solving exercises. DrillBooks retains rights in the original exercise wording, notebook design, supplied datasets, examples, hints, explanations, and official solutions. [Product Licence](https://www.drillbooks.codes/license.html)

### Does lifetime access include future updates or permanent cloud storage?

No such guarantee is published. ## Cancellation and refunds

### Can I cancel an order?

You may request cancellation before payment is successfully completed. After digital access has been delivered, accessed, or downloaded, an order generally cannot be cancelled for a change of mind. [Cancellation and Refund Policy](https://www.drillbooks.codes/refund-policy.html)

### When might a refund be approved?

The policy says a full or appropriate refund may be approved for duplicate captured payment; captured payment with unresolved failure to deliver; a materially incorrect product that cannot be corrected; files that are corrupted or materially inaccessible when no working replacement can be provided; or where required by Indian consumer law. Contact support for review; this is not an automatic eligibility decision. [Cancellation and Refund Policy](https://www.drillbooks.codes/refund-policy.html)

### When are refunds normally unavailable?

The policy says refunds are normally unavailable for change of mind after receiving or downloading the files, no longer needing the product, expecting guaranteed outcomes, a device that fails disclosed requirements, lack of basic software to open notebooks or ZIPs, an incorrect email address that can be corrected, a resolvable third-party software issue, or a licence breach. The policy also says this does not remove consumer remedies that cannot legally be excluded. [Cancellation and Refund Policy](https://www.drillbooks.codes/refund-policy.html)

### How do I request a refund?

Email `drillbooks.support@gmail.com` with subject **“Refund Request – Razorpay Order ID”**. Include your name, checkout email, Razorpay order or payment ID, payment date and amount, a clear explanation, and relevant error screenshots with sensitive credentials hidden. Report delivery or file problems preferably within seven calendar days so records can be investigated promptly. [Cancellation and Refund Policy](https://www.drillbooks.codes/refund-policy.html)

### How long does an approved refund take?

Approved refunds are normally returned to the original payment method through Razorpay. After DrillBooks initiates the refund, the bank/payment provider controls posting time; the policy says it commonly takes approximately 5–10 business days. [Cancellation and Refund Policy](https://www.drillbooks.codes/refund-policy.html)

### What if one payment succeeded and another is pending?

The policy says to wait for the bank and Razorpay to update the status. Failed or reversed payments may be returned automatically by the payment provider. If two payments were both successfully captured for one intended order, contact support for a duplicate-payment review. [Cancellation and Refund Policy](https://www.drillbooks.codes/refund-policy.html)
